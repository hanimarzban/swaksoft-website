About

A software brand built on craft

Swak is short for Swiss Army Knife. A tool for every need, a need for every tool.

Swak is short for Swiss Army Knife. That is where it all started.

The Swiss Army knife has always been a fascinating object. Not because it is the best knife, or the best screwdriver, or the best pair of scissors. It is none of those things. But it carries a tool for almost every situation you find yourself in, and it does so without demanding that you carry twelve separate things. Every blade earns its place. Nothing is redundant. The whole thing slips into a pocket.

That idea became the founding logic of SwakSoft. Build a software company the same way: not one product that tries to be everything, but a growing catalog of tools, each one built for a real purpose, each one useful enough to stand on its own. A startup where the question is never what should we remove, but what else do people actually need.

SwakSoft was founded in Azna, Iran, by a developer who had spent years building software and had grown increasingly convinced that most tools fell short in the same ways: too complicated to start, too limited to finish the job, and too expensive to try without commitment. The goal was to build differently.

Today the catalog covers desktop software, web applications, mobile apps, and AI-powered tools. But the idea that started SwakSoft has no natural stopping point. A Swiss Army knife does not stop at ten blades because ten felt like enough. In the years ahead, we plan to expand into robotics and the Internet of Things, building software that works alongside hardware and connects the physical world to the digital one. Every domain we enter will follow the same logic: find a real need, build a tool that meets it properly, and ship it when it is ready.

What we believe

Software should work. Not impress, not onboard, not explain itself. Just work.

We believe the best software is the kind you forget is there. It does its job, stays out of your way, and never asks you to read a manual before you can use it. That belief shapes every decision we make, from how features are designed to what gets cut before shipping.

We keep data local whenever possible. We build for people who speak languages other than English. We offer something real for free, without a time limit. These are not features: they are commitments about the kind of company SwakSoft is.

When something is ready, it ships. When it is not, it does not.
