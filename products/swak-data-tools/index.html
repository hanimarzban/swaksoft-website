Common questions

Does Swak Data Tools work on Mac?+
Yes. Swak Data Tools works on Excel for Windows (2016+) and Excel for Mac (2019+), as well as Microsoft 365 on both platforms. A Microsoft 365 subscription is not required.
Does Swak Data Tools work with Chinese Office (国内版 Office)?+
Yes. Swak Data Tools is fully compatible with Chinese editions of Microsoft Office and Microsoft 365. Installation uses a direct manifest file, no Microsoft Store access required. Simply download the setup file from this page and follow the installation steps. The full UI is available in Chinese (中文). See installation guide for China
Does my data leave my computer?+
Almost never. All 215 tools, including all ML models and Statistics tools, run entirely on your local machine. The only exception is the 18 AI Insights tools, which send only the selected cell range to the AI model to generate a response.
Can I use Swak Data Tools in my own language?+
Yes. Swak Data Tools supports 9 languages: English, Persian (فارسی), Arabic (العربية), French, Spanish, German, Chinese (中文), Russian (Русский), and Hindi (हिंदी). The entire UI and all AI Insights responses adapt to your selected language.
How does billing work?+
All plans are one-time purchases for the chosen period (1 month, 3 months, or 6 months). There is no automatic renewal or recurring charge. When your license period ends, your tools revert to the free tier and you can choose to renew manually.
What is the welcome AI credit?+
Every new license — including the free plan — receives a one-time welcome credit of 875,000 AI tokens, powered by Claude AI. This is not a monthly allowance; it is a single credit that lets you explore all 18 AI Insights tools before spending anything. Once used, you can top up your token balance anytime from the license portal at swaksoft.com/license.
Which 23 tools are free?+
Free tools include: Remove Duplicates, Fill Missing Values, Sort Data, Search & Highlight, Pivot Table, Transpose, Linear Regression, K-Means Clustering, Explain Data, and more, all permanently unlocked, no credit card required.
How does the Pro license work?+
A Pro Individual license is tied to one device at a time. You receive a license key that activates all 215 tools on your machine. If you switch computers, contact support to transfer your license. Corporate plans support multiple simultaneous devices.
